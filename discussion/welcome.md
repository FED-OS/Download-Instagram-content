# Welcome to FED-GRAM Discussions 👋

Thanks for stopping by! This is the community space for FED-GRAM — questions,
ideas, and conversation that **isn't** a bug report or a formal feature
request.

## Before you post

1. **Search** existing discussions — your question may already be answered.
2. Check the [FAQ](../FAQ.md) and [docs](../SUMMARY.md) — many answers live there.
3. Bugs → [Issues](https://github.com/FED-OS/FED-GRAM/issues) with the bug template.
4. Concrete feature requests → [Issues](https://github.com/FED-OS/FED-GRAM/issues)
   with the feature template.
5. Security issues → **do not** post publicly; see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Categories at a glance

- **💬 General** — anything FED-GRAM.
- **🙋 Q&A** — how do I…?
- **💡 Ideas** — early-stage brainstorming.
- **🙏 Show and tell** — your deployments, custom engines, use cases.
- **🔧 Engines** — scraping strategies and platform-markup changes.

## Rules

Be kind, be specific, and don't share private content or secrets. We follow
the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). FED-GRAM is maintained by
volunteers — please be patient with response times.

Glad to have you here! 💛
