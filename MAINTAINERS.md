reviewing pull requests, triaging issues, cutting releases, and keeping the
scraping engines working as platforms evolve.

## Responsibilities

A maintainer is expected to:

- Review pull requests within a reasonable timeframe (target: within 7 days).
- Triage new issues, label them, and close duplicates/stale ones.
- Keep scraping engines working as platforms change their markup.
- Ensure `requirements.txt`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and docs stay in sync with code.
- Respond to security reports privately per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Be respectful and uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

FED-GRAM follows a merit-based model. Consistent, high-quality contributors who
understand the architecture may be invited to join the maintainer team. There
is no formal application — sustained contribution is the path. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the decision process.

## Stepping down

Maintainers may step down at any time by notifying the lead maintainer. We ask
for a brief handover note for any area you own (e.g. "the Pinterest engine
needs a markup check every few months"). Former maintainers are thanked in
[AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md).
